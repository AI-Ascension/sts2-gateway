# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive,
and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback; and
- accepting responsibility and learning from mistakes.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks,
unwelcome sexual attention, publication of private information, or other conduct that would be
considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct and may take
appropriate corrective action in response to behavior they consider unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions
that violate this Code of Conduct. They may provide reasons for moderation decisions when doing so
does not expose private information.

## Scope and reporting

This Code applies in project spaces and wherever an individual represents the project. Report
abusive or unacceptable behavior through the repository's private maintainer contact or security
channel. Do not include credentials, save data, personal paths, or exploit details in a public issue.
